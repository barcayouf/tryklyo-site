What klyo does

  • Snap & log meals — capture a photo and get an estimate of calories, protein, carbs, and fat.
  • Personal targets — set a goal to lose, maintain, or gain weight, and klyo calculates your daily targets.
  • AI nutrition coach — ask questions and get guidance based on your recent intake and progress.
  • Meal timing around your day — with your permission, klyo reads your calendar to suggest eating windows that fit your schedule.
  • Weight & progress tracking — log your weight over time and watch the trend, not the noise.
