Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 May 2026 · Last updated: 14 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how klyo ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the klyo mobile application. By using klyo you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

klyo is operated by an independent developer based in Israel ("the developer"). Contact: info@tryklyo.io.

2. Information we collect

We collect the minimum needed to operate the app:

Account information

  • Email address (for authentication).
  • Authentication identifiers from third-party providers if you sign in with Apple or Google.

Profile and health-related information you enter

  • Name (optional), date of birth or age, sex, height.
  • Body weight and weight history.
  • Activity level, goal (lose / maintain / gain weight), goal pace.

Nutrition and food intake

  • Meals you log: text descriptions, estimated calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, serving size.
  • Photos of meals you choose to capture or upload, used to estimate nutrition. Photos are stored in your account.

Schedule data (optional)

  • If you grant calendar access, we read your upcoming calendar events to time meal-window suggestions. We store only event start time, end time, and title. We do not store event location or attendees.

Coaching conversations

  • Messages you send to the in-app coach and the coach's responses are stored in your account so you can revisit them.

Notifications

  • Push notification token, used to deliver meal-window reminders. You can disable notifications in the OS settings.

We do not collect

  • Precise location.
  • Contacts.
  • Browsing history.
  • Advertising identifiers.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the core service: calculate targets, log meals, track progress, suggest eating windows.
  • To generate AI coaching responses based on your messages and recent context.
  • To send notifications you have opted into.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To detect abuse and keep the service secure.

We do not use your data for advertising and do not sell your data.

5. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all your data is removed from our database immediately. Backups containing your data are purged on a rolling 30-day cycle.

Calendar events we read are never persisted beyond the short period required to compute suggestions.

6. Your rights

You may, at any time:

  • Access your data — visible in the app, or by request.
  • Correct your data — directly in the app.
  • Delete your account and all associated data — Settings → Delete account, or by email.
  • Export your data — by email request.
  • Object to specific processing or withdraw consent for optional features (camera, calendar, notifications) in the OS settings.

To exercise any right, email info@tryklyo.io. We respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. If you are in Israel, you may contact the Privacy Protection Authority.

7. Children

klyo is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures:

  • All traffic encrypted in transit (TLS).
  • Database row-level security: each user can only access their own rows.
  • Passwords are never stored — authentication is handled by Supabase Auth.
  • Limited internal access on a need-to-know basis.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. International transfers

Your data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt). When you use AI coaching, message content is processed by Anthropic in the United States under standard contractual clauses.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be notified in-app. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest revision.

11. Contact

Questions, requests, complaints: info@tryklyo.io.
